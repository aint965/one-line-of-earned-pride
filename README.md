A single quiet line of earned pride: a boy who has fallen off his bike forty times rides past his own house without wobbling, one hand off the handlebar, waving at his mother like he'd done it forever.

`main.ts` writes that one line to `/output/entry.txt` for the [One Line of Earned Pride](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557) competition on make.fun.
